About Me

I'm Tom, a second-year BSc Computer Science student at the University of Surrey. I enjoy turning ideas into practical software and understanding how the systems behind everyday applications work.

My interest in programming began with Java at school, where I created games such as Pong and Snake. Since then, I've developed projects ranging from a desktop recipe manager to a CPU scheduling simulator, a REST API and a deployed URL shortener. My experience includes Java, Python, SQL, Swing and Spring Boot.

During an IT Technician internship at CNM Estates, I gained practical experience supporting office technology, improving website features and working with an intranet and Microsoft Access. This helped me connect my technical interests with the needs of people using technology at work.

Before university, I completed the International Baccalaureate, studying Computer Science, Business Management and Economics at Higher Level. These subjects developed my interest in both building software and understanding the problems it can help people and organisations solve.

I'm continuing to develop my skills through university coursework and independent projects, with a focus on building useful applications and becoming a more thoughtful, capable developer.
